Privacy policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

WallHub is a private display used only by the members of one household.

What it accesses

With each family member's permission, WallHub reads their Google Calendar events (read-only) so they can be shown on the family's wall screen at home.

How the data is used and stored

  • Calendar data is used only to display events on the household's own screen.
  • It is stored only on the household's own device on the home network, and only as long as needed to show it.
  • It is never sold, shared, or sent to any third party, and is not used for advertising or analytics.

Removing access

Anyone can remove WallHub's access at any time from their Google Account under Security → Third-party apps & services.

WallHub's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
